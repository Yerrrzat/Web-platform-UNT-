## 4.4 Analysis

- **The market is crowded for general UNT preparation but thin for Informatics.** None of the five systems describes a dedicated, deep Informatics product. The NTC trial is free and official, so it is a strong baseline for *format*, but no public source shows that it explains each item.
- **Claims about modern item types could not be verified** for any competitor (C6 = n/s). This is noted as an *assumption* about competitor weakness rather than a fact, and it will be checked during the pilot by asking users to compare.
- **Price:** iTest shows that students accept cheap subscriptions (about 1,500 KZT per month). Course platforms charge far more. The proposed price sits at the test-bank level, not the course level.
- **Free official alternative:** because the NTC trial is free, the platform must win on *explanations, Informatics depth and repeated practice*, not on format alone.

## 4.5 Why the proposed solution is needed

The proposed platform does not compete with full courses. It fills a niche: **an Informatics-only, Kazakh-first practice-test service with item-level explanations and topic analytics, at test-bank prices.** This is supported by research on retrieval practice and elaborated feedback (Section 3) and by the gaps in table 4.3. The weakest part of the argument, user willingness to pay, is treated as a hypothesis and tested in the pilot (Section 9).
